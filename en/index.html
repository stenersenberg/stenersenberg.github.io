Frequently asked questions

Why use a baby names app?

A baby names app brings structure to your name finding process. It helps you find names you would never think of yourself through personal suggestions, gives you always updated popular name lists, and makes it easy to sort and filter names to your needs. It is also fun to search together with others.

How much does it cost?

Find Baby Names is free to use. On Android the app is completely free, and on iOS you can do a lot without paying. Browsing through the names for one country is free. On iOS you also have the option to pay to unlock some extra features.

Can I find names together with my partner?

Yes. Both of you can go through names on your own phones, and the names you both like show up as matches. That makes it easy to find names you agree on.

Which countries are covered?

The app currently includes names from Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, the USA, England, Scotland, Northern Ireland, Brazil and France.
